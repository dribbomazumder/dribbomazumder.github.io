About me

Numbers by study, stories by passion.

I'm a student in the Department of Statistics at Chittagong College. Statistics trains me to find the pattern inside the noise — and I bring that same habit to creative work: finding the story inside the footage.

With about a year of hands-on experience in video and photo editing, I focus on clean pacing, honest storytelling and clear communication. I'm comfortable working in Bangla, English, Japanese and Hindi, which helps me connect with a wide range of audiences.
